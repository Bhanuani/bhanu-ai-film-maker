Questions, answered

Before you step into the studio.

What is CINEMOTION STUDIO?

CINEMOTION STUDIO is a filmmaking reference workspace for cinematic camera movements, angles, shots, lighting, atmosphere, lenses and prompt guidance.

Who can use the studio?

It is designed for filmmakers, video creators, editors, visual storytellers and creators developing image or video prompts.

What is inside the Director Vault?

The current application includes references across camera movements, camera angles, camera shots, cinematic lighting, atmosphere, camera lens and master prompts.

Can I copy and use the prompts?

Yes. Open a reference in the existing application to review its prompt details and use the available copy actions.

Does CINEMOTION include an AI assistant?

Yes. The existing application includes a Gemini assistant for questions about cinematic prompts and visual direction.

How do I access my account?

Select Enter Studio. Your existing application will open with its current login, registration and mobile OTP options.
